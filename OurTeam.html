Empowering Student Life

Our committee brings together passionate individuals across six sub-committees
to build a vibrant, inclusive, and supportive environment for all SIT students.

Ambassador

Represent the club and school at official events, guide new students, and promote the SIT Club during public engagements.

Social and Sports

Foster camaraderie through bonding events, sports activities, and wellness programs to build lasting friendships.

Community and Student Support

Lead community service efforts, promote inclusivity, and provide peer support and outreach to students in need.

Secretary

Maintain documentation, meeting minutes, and internal communication to ensure everything runs smoothly.

Treasury

Manage finances, track expenses, handle reimbursements, and prepare funding proposals with transparency.

Publicity

Create posters, manage social media, and capture event highlights to raise awareness and promote club activities.
