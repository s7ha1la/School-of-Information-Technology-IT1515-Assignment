Featured Events
SIGs x SIT Tech Week 2024
A Workshop organised by SIGs x SIT for Tech Week 2024 where its prerequisites involves the
usage of Python, Mongo DB, VS Code. The Agenda was to introduce students of IT to the different
applications used in a Database where it involves Web Application Demo, Backend: Flask and APIs and
Database with MongoDB.



SIT x SIGs x SCS Career Sharing 2024
A School Event for Career Sharing 2024 tailored with different industries where
it opens students to different opportunities in the industry such as AWS, J.P. Morgan and many more.
This helps students to navigate their way through the Tech Industry upon graduation.



SIT Polytechnic to University Sharing 2023
A School Event for students interested in pursuing their studies further to university
Different Universities Representatives come together such as NUS, NTU, Yale and SIT
to give their sharing on pursuing further studies as well as their tips to enter to their ideal schools.



NYP SIT x SCS Youth Career Sharing 2023
An Event where all Alumni's gathers sharing their experience at NYP SIT and displaying their
entrepreneurship after their education. Some came out to have their own Tech Companies and are successful.



Code Overflow Github Pre-Workshop 2023
A Github Pre-Workshop, where it teaches students on the knowledge of using Github
at the start of school year, helping to expose Year 1 students to Github which is
beneficial for their 3 Years of Studies in NYP as it is a common IDE used for coding and programming.
